Session overview

About this session

3,000m · Freestyle only. Continuous swims are manual: swim the distance, then tap NEXT. For interval sets, START begins the send-off countdown; the app beeps at zero and advances to the next rep.

The send-offs are editable examples, not targets to force. Adjust them to your normal training pace. In a warm pool, keep the effort controlled, drink between sections, and stop if you feel dizzy, nauseous or unusually overheated.

LaneTimer · Keep your phone somewhere safe and dry on poolside.
